SCALE SNAP {{VERSION}}  -  install in under a minute (Ableton Live 11 Suite, Mac or Windows)

1. UNZIP ScaleSnap-Ableton.zip. You get a folder called "Scale Snap" with two devices:
     Scale Snap.amxd         MIDI effect: keeps what you play in key
     Scale Snap Detect.amxd  audio effect: finds the key of an audio track

2. KEEP THEM: drag the whole "Scale Snap" folder into Live's browser, onto
   Places > User Library.
   (Or in Finder / Explorer: put it in ~/Music/Ableton/User Library/ on a Mac,
   Documents\Ableton\User Library\ on Windows.)

3. USE THEM: open User Library > Scale Snap in the browser.
     - Drag "Scale Snap" onto a MIDI track, in front of the instrument.
     - Drag "Scale Snap Detect" onto an audio track.
   No Terminal, no extra files, no second track. Each device is a single file.

Quick try without installing: drag Scale Snap.amxd straight from the unzipped
folder onto a MIDI track (before the instrument).

Problems? Scale Snap needs Max for Live (included in Live 11 Suite). If a device
stays empty, see m4l/INSTALL.md in the repository.
